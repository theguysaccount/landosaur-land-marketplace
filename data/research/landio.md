# LANDIO public-product audit

Research date: October 1, 2026. Scope: public homepage and expanded filters; one complete property detail and map-layer menu; official FAQ and agent/feature pages. No signed-in agent workflow, analytics accuracy, financial performance, listing ownership, or independent parcel/title verification was tested.

LANDIO is a useful benchmark for property presentation and distribution. Its FAQ describes a real-estate marketing business with paid placements, referral relationships, and a limited number of properties held by an affiliated company. It says LANDIO itself is not a brokerage. These are the company's statements, not an independent corporate or licensing audit. [Official FAQ](https://www.landio.com/faq)

| Area | Public evidence observed | Implication for the proposed company |
|---|---|---|
| Search | Map/list/split views; price, acreage, price per acre, state/county, city/ZIP, home, status and payment filters; radius, drive-time and drawn-area controls | These are baseline features to meet. Search should also answer intended-use and cost questions. |
| Map | 3D, topography and weather controls; property map menu includes surface water, wells and public lands | Additional overlays alone are a weak differentiation. Join layers to parcel-specific evidence and decisions. |
| Inventory | The default loaded browser view displayed 91 matching properties and initially rendered 50 | A dated observation of that view, not a company-wide inventory audit or market-share estimate. |
| Buyer engagement | Save, sharing, representative contact and a chat entry point | Saved searches and chat alone will not differentiate a new site. |
| Property facts | Sample page contains APN, zoning, utilities, access, taxes, title and elevation | Do not claim LANDIO lacks these fields. Improve provenance, verification and comparability. |

Source: direct browser observation of [LANDIO](https://www.landio.com/) and the [Taney County sample](https://www.landio.com/listing/mo_taney_00001). Controls were inspected; every possible filter combination was not tested.

The feature page advertises agent credential review, 3D/KML presentation, galleries, video and 360-degree tours, lead analytics, and direct inquiries. Its onboarding describes application, review, and account activation. These are advertised capabilities; private workflows were not tested. [Feature page](https://www.landio.com/feature)

**The most valuable gaps to investigate are evidence, valuation, and execution.** The following were not found in the inspected public surfaces; this is not proof that LANDIO has no private or forthcoming version.

| Priority | Capability not observed | Proposed implementation | How to prove it is useful |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cross-market identity and price history | One parcel record with source-specific listings, prices, statuses, rights and timestamps | Blindly review duplicate merges and stale-listing removals |
| 1 | Calibrated estimate with sold evidence | Comparable sales, adjustments, range, model date, local error and abstention | Test against later verified sales in counties withheld from training |
| 1 | All-in opportunity ranking | Asking price plus closing, access/site work, carry and exit costs against a conservative value | Compare top-ranked results with independent professional diligence |
| 1 | A document-backed parcel dossier | Link each decisive claim to a dated record, report or accountable professional | Measure unresolved facts before and after a paid report |
| 1 | Intended-use screening | Separate current permissions from proposals: home, RV, camping, farm, subdivision or commercial use | County/local review of sampled decisions |
| 2 | A fulfilled services workflow | Scope, quote, schedule, deliverable, acceptance and updated parcel evidence | Completion time, quote accuracy and repeat customers |
| 2 | Professional acquisition workspace | Saved buy boxes, team review, tasks, offers, vendor orders and deal room | Weekly repeat usage and time saved per reviewed parcel |
| 2 | Financing comparison with full terms | Cash price, down payment, rate, term, balloon, fees, total payments and title timing | Buyer comprehension and lender-confirmed terms |
| 3 | Landowner operating account | Leases, renewals, taxes, inspections and management history after acquisition | Retention after the purchase event |

**A concrete example of the difference.** The Taney County listing advertised 10 acres for $50,000, an access easement, agricultural zoning, well and septic not installed, and power along a road. Those are listing assertions. A useful next step is a parcel dossier with the easement instrument and route, local confirmation of the intended use, septic feasibility, a power-extension quote, terrain constraints and appropriate closed sales. The current research does not establish the parcel's market value or claim that it is underpriced. [Sample listing](https://www.landio.com/listing/mo_taney_00001)

The proposed service should turn unanswered questions into trackable work. For that sample, it would show “access document requested,” “well/septic budget pending,” and “valuation unavailable until sufficient evidence,” while permitting the user to request the relevant professional work. That transition from attractive listing to evidence-backed decision is the product opportunity.

**LANDIO can also be a potential distribution or content partner.** A commercially sensible proposal would exchange qualified demand, verified property evidence, or seller tools for expressly permitted listing access. No partnership, API, feed export or republication permission has been established by this research.

**Competitive check beyond LANDIO.** LandSearch already has a [land services directory](https://www.landsearch.com/services), a [loan calculator](https://www.landsearch.com/loan-calculator), and [price-per-acre pages](https://www.landsearch.com/price). The latter states that its pricing reflects current marketplace inventory and is not intended for market analysis. These existing features make workflow quality and valuation validity more important than merely combining directory, map and calculator tabs.

**Feed direction matters.** LandSearch's [feed program](https://www.landsearch.com/feed) accepts approved inbound listings. It is not evidence of a feed granting another company permission to republish LandSearch's inventory. Its [terms](https://www.landsearch.com/corp/terms) require express written permission for automated extraction, apart from a limited indexing exception. A proposed commercial aggregation program needs its own access rights.
